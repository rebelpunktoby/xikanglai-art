About 關於

For more than three decades Xikang Lai has looked for the moment a body holds still without losing its motion. He studied sculpture at the Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts, which holds his graduation work Civilized Ruins (1990), and has taught in Guangzhou ever since — first at a vocational school, then as a visiting lecturer and professor. His studio works move between dancers, yogis and turning figures in bronze, cupronickel and marble; his public commissions stand in parks, schools and memorial halls. Since 2022 the Gate series has carried the same search into geometry. He is a member of the Guangdong Artists Association.三十多年來，賴錫康一直在尋找那個身體靜止、卻不失動勢的瞬間。他畢業於廣州美術學院雕塑系，畢業作品《廢墟》（1990）現藏學院；此後一直在廣州任教——先在職業中學，後任多所院校客座講師及教授。他的架上作品在舞者、瑜伽與迴轉的人體之間往返，以青銅、白銅與漢白玉塑造；公共作品則立於公園、學校與紀念館。自2022年起，「門系列」把這份追尋帶入幾何。廣東省美術家協會會員。

Statement and perspective 自述與評論

Xikang Lai, 2019賴錫康

“I inherit tradition, and within limits I overturn it. My work takes up figures and themes: from historical figures I try to draw out the spirit particular to each, and through the shaping of form to make a particular beauty. From literature I take nourishment, trying to turn ordinary things into something extraordinary and to build a bridge from appearance to the inner life — a poetic image. I hold to the beauty all people share and try to dig deeper for it, however disheartening what we see today may be. To begin from a traditional sense of beauty and give it modern value is extremely hard, but I am willing to try, hoping that through craft I can find, from more angles, what is able to move people. As the Polish poet Adam Zagajewski said: try to praise the mutilated world.”

「我繼承傳統又有限度地顛覆傳統，作品涉及人物和主題等方面的創作。這些作品傾向於從歷史人物中提煉各自的精神特質，通過形的塑造創作出特定的美。另一方面，我從文學中吸取營養，試圖將普通的事物轉換成特別的東西，並架建起一座從表象通往內心的橋樑，即詩意的圖像。我崇尚人類共有的美，並試圖更深入地挖掘，即使是今天我們看到那樣的令人沮喪。當然，由傳統的審美作基礎去挖掘美使之具備現代審美價值是極其困難的，但我願意嘗試，期望通過技術手段從更多的角度發現並塑造足以感動人的方面。如波蘭詩人亞當・扎嘎耶夫斯基言：試著讚美這遭損毀的世界。」

Li Gongming, 2015李公明

“Lai’s studio sculpture speaks above all in a figurative, lyrical language; he is especially gifted at holding a graceful instant of the body still as an image to be contemplated. In his work, bending and stretching, leaning and balance, strength and softness, tension and release arise naturally in form and in meaning, setting one another off and flowing together. Representative works such as The Beauty of Curved Stretching, Balance and Shi show a reflective understanding of the body’s beauty and a mature command of space. His apt choice of materials and refined craft raise them further.”

「賴錫康的架上雕塑作品以具象性與抒情性為其主要語言風格，尤其擅長把人體的優美體態的瞬間定格為特定的審美欣賞形象。在其作品中，人體的曲與伸、欹側與平衡、剛健與柔和、緊繃與舒鬆等形、意要素生發自然，對比映襯，融匯貫通。如《曲伸之美》、《平衡》、《釋》等代表性作品，表現了作者對人體審美的思辨性與成熟的空間造型能力。同時，作者對材質的恰當選擇和精湛的加工處理技巧也提升了作品的觀賞與收藏價值。」
